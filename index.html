❓ How to Play

  1. Draw a chip from your bag. Every chip stirs the potion further up the cauldron.
  2. The higher the potion, the more points and coins the round pays — and it climbs much faster near the top.
  3. 🫧 Fizzroot fills the fizz gauge. Go over the limit and the lid comes off: the round's points are gone.
  4. You keep the coins either way — so a blow-up early is a shopping trip, and a blow-up in the last round is a disaster.
  5. Stop whenever you like: 🍯 Bottle it banks everything the round has made.
  6. Between rounds the apothecary sells new chips. They go into your bag for the rest of the brew — so every purchase makes everything else rarer. Buy fewer, better things.
  7. Five rounds. Beat the recipe's target for stars, and spend 🔮 essence in the Larder on perks that last forever.
